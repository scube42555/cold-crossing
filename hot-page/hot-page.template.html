Midnight hot page

Runs on the online (hot) machine, served by tools/hot_server.py on this computer only. It watches the cold wallet's address, hands its snapshot to the cold machine, and has the sponsor wallet pay the fee and submit the transfer the cold machine signed. It starts the same scripts you would run by hand (hot-watch, sponsor-submit); no secret is typed into or shown on this page.
